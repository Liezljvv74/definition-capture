Questions

Which languages can I use it for?

Any language you can type in. Your lists, your words, your way.

Who can see what I save?

Only you. Everything you save stays inside your account.

How do the flashcards work?

You type the meaning. Get it right and the card comes back later. Get it wrong and it returns sooner.

Can I keep a copy of my data?

Yes. Back everything up to a file whenever you like.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Open it in your phone's browser and your notebook is there.
